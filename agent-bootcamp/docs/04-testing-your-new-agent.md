# Testing your new custom Post-IPL Health agent
The hard part is done! Now you get to test your new agent.

## Overview
All of your connections, tools, and IPL agent have been uploaded to your watsonx orchestrate instance. Now it is time to make sure your agent behaves as expected, using your connection's credentials and your tools' capabilities to validate that your conifgurations and subsystems are what you expect after an IPL.


### 1. Login to watsonx Orchestrate
Login to your watsonx Orchestrate from IBM Cloud. Click on the Hamburger icon in the upper left hand corner and navigate to the **Build** tab. You should see your IPL_Validator agent listed. Click on the tile.

### 2. Test your agent
In the chat window, type in "run IPL checks" or "validate my IPL" or any variation of that phrase. You should see a response from your agent, and a list of the checks that were run, and the results of each check.

### 3. Optional- Publish your agent
If you are pleased with your agent, you can publish your agent for live work! In this bootcamp, that doesn't mean much but in your organization, this would make the agent avialable for use by your wider teams. 
To publish your agent, just click on the **Deploy** button in the upper right hand corner.


## Congratulations! You have successfully created a custom Post-IPL Health agent!
